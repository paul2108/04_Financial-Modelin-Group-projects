notebook's cells. Users can switch the trend filter off, change the moving-average length, the risk window, the weighting
scheme (risk parity, inverse volatility, equal weights), trading costs, the benchmark, the start year, the asset classes held and the
machine-learning overlay of Section~9 (variants A--D of Table~\ref{tab:ml}),
and immediately see cumulative performance, drawdowns, weights and all measures against the benchmark. With default settings
