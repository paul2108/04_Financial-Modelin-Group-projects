\textbf{Source and sample.} Daily prices from Yahoo Finance (\texttt{yfinance}) for the six ETFs, AGG, the S\&P 500 total
return index (\texttt{\textasciicircum SP500TR}) and the 13-week T-bill yield (\texttt{\textasciicircum IRX}), from
\res{data_start} to \res{data_end}. The youngest ETF (DBC, February 2006) and the 252-day risk window set the first decision
date to \res{first_decision}, giving \res{sample_months} monthly returns from \res{sample_start} to \res{sample_end}, including
the financial crisis, the Covid crash and the 2022 rate shock.

\textbf{Adjustments and calculations.} Prices are adjusted for splits and distributions, so returns are total returns after
fund fees. Month-end values are the last close; returns are simple, so portfolio returns are exact weighted sums. The cash
return in month $t$ is the \texttt{\textasciicircum IRX} yield at the end of $t-1$ divided by 12 (average \res{rf_mean} p.a.).
Beta and Jensen's alpha come from regressing excess returns on S\&P 500 total-return excess returns, with Newey-West
$t$-statistics \citep{newey1987}; the other measures are defined below Table~\ref{tab:performance}. Costs of \res{cost_bps}
apply to every unit of turnover (sum of absolute changes against drifted weights) for strategy and benchmark alike. Every
number is computed in the notebook; clicking a value opens the section that computes it.
