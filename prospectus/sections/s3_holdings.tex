The portfolio holds one large, liquid ETF per asset class (Table~\ref{tab:holdings}). The asset classes are those
of \citet{faber2007}, extended by gold. They were chosen because they respond differently to growth and inflation
shocks (stocks and REITs to growth, Treasuries to recessions, commodities and gold to inflation), not because of
their past returns. Prices of the ETFs already reflect their fees.
